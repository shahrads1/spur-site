Spur / child safety

Child safety standards

Spur (developer: Spur Social Corp.) has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everyone who uses Spur, on every platform.

Adults only

Spur is for people 18 and older. Every account confirms its age at sign-up and verifies a phone number. Accounts we learn belong to someone under 18 are removed, and "Under 18" is a report reason that is flagged urgent for our safety team.

What is prohibited

Any content or behaviour that sexualises, grooms, exploits or endangers a minor is prohibited, including sharing, requesting or linking to CSAM, and using Spur to arrange contact with a minor. This is covered in our Community Guidelines and Terms.

How we prevent and detect it

Uploaded photos are automatically screened before they are shown, including with Microsoft PhotoDNA to detect known child sexual abuse imagery, and with automated moderation for explicit content. Members can report any person, message, plan or event from inside the app; reports are reviewed by a person.

What we do

When we find or are told about CSAE, we remove the content, permanently ban the accounts involved (and block them from signing up again), preserve the evidence as the law requires, and report it to the appropriate authorities, including Cybertip.ca (Canadian Centre for Child Protection), the U.S. National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and law enforcement. We comply with all applicable child safety laws.

How to report

In the app: use Report on the person, message, plan or event and choose "Under 18" or the reason that fits. You can also email our child safety contact at hello@spur.surf. If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 first. You can also report directly to Cybertip.ca or NCMEC's CyberTipline.

Get in touch

Spur is built in Toronto by Spur Social Corp. Whatever it's about, a real person reads it.

Join the iOS beta

Spur is in a closed beta, Toronto-first. Want in? Email hello@spur.surf and say hi — or just open Spur in your browser right now.

Put your venue on Spur

Bars, courts, studios, karting tracks — if people can meet up there, we want you on the map. Email hello@spur.surf with your venue's name and what you'd like to run.

Safety & reports

Something happened that shouldn't have? Use the in-app report tools (they reach us fastest), or email hello@spur.surf. Reports are reviewed by a human.

Privacy requests

To access, correct, or delete your personal information (see our privacy policy), email hello@spur.surf. We respond within the timeframes required by law.

Three steps, zero group-chat purgatory

No event pages to build, no polls about which weekend. Say the thing, do the thing.

1

Say what you're up for

"Volleyball at Woodbine, 6pm." That's the whole post. Add a venue or a time window if you want.

2

People nearby jump in

Your post shows up for people around you who are into the same thing. They tap in, you see who's coming.

3

Everyone actually shows up

Plans on Spur are tonight or this week — not "we should hang sometime." Reliability is tracked, so flaking has a cost.

Built so people show up

Every plan app dies the same death: everyone's "interested," nobody comes. Spur's whole design fights that — showing up is the product.

Real people only

Every account is phone-verified — no bots, no burners. Hosts build a visible track record.

Deposits beat flaking

Hosts can ask for a small refundable deposit. Show up and it comes right back. Ghost, and it doesn't.

Safety isn't an afterthought

Block and report tools, photo moderation, and community guidelines with teeth. Meet in public places, always.

Made in Toronto

Spur is built by Spur Social Corp., a Canadian federal corporation headquartered in Toronto. We're starting with one city — ours — and getting spontaneous, in-person hangouts right before going anywhere else.

The app is currently in a closed iOS beta. Want in, or want your venue on Spur? Write us: hello@spur.surf

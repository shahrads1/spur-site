Get outside, do things.

Spur shows you real plans happening near you tonight. Bocce at Christie Pits. Badminton in High Park. Drinks in Kensington. Join one, or start your own.

The problem

No more group chat limbo.

Six people, forty messages, nobody commits. Spur skips the negotiation. You see plans that already have a time and a place, and you either join or you don’t.

The places

Real venues, all over Toronto.

Christie Pits, Cherry Beach, Trinity Bellwoods, Kensington, the Islands. Hosts pick a real place at a real time, so you always know exactly where you’re going.

The timing

Tonight, not next month.

Most plans go up the same day. Open Spur after work and there’s usually something on within a couple of kilometres.

Ready when you are.

Pick something happening tonight and go. Worst case, you meet four people and play bocce badly.
